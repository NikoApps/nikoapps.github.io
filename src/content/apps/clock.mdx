<Section id="features" title="What it does">

- **Tap to hear the time.** Tap the clock or press the big button. Tap twice for the date.
- **Shake to hear it.** Works with the screen off and the phone locked. Shake again for the date.
- **Chimes.** Says the time by itself every hour, half hour or quarter hour, with its own volume.
- **Quiet at night.** No chimes during quiet hours or Do Not Disturb. Asking still works.
- **Day clock.** Turns the screen into a large "Tuesday morning" clock with the full date, for people who lose track of the day. Dark at night, and can stay on the lock screen while charging.
- **Nepali date and a note.** Can show the Bikram Sambat date and a short note from a helper, and read both after the date.
- **Good morning.** Can say good morning and the date at 6, 7 or 8.
- **Tiles and widgets.** Hear the time or the date from quick settings, or from a home screen widget.
- **Speaks like a person.** "It's quarter to four in the afternoon", not "fifteen forty-five".
- **Set up by a helper.** A short setup on first open. Settings can be locked with a PIN.
- **Keeps speaking.** Warns when a phone setting may stop it, and shows when it last spoke by itself.

</Section>

<Section id="privacy" title="Privacy">

No internet permission, nothing collected, no ads, no account. [How Niko handles privacy](/privacy/).

<Question q="What does it keep on the phone?">

Its settings: voice language, speed and pitch, chime times, quiet hours, shake settings, text size, day clock and morning greeting choices, the time it last spoke, and a short note if a helper writes one. A helper PIN is kept only as a one-way hash, never as the numbers themselves.

</Question>

<Question q="What is each permission for?">

- **Alarms:** to say the time on the minute, and good morning at the time a helper picks.
- **Run at startup:** to start chimes and shake again after the phone restarts.
- **Foreground service and keep awake:** to listen for a shake and to finish speaking with the screen off.
- **Notifications:** to show that shake listening is on, as Android requires.
- **Vibrate:** for the optional buzz before speaking.

It has no permission for the internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage or phone calls.

</Question>

<Question q="Does the voice send anything anywhere?">

Niko Clock doesn't. The voice comes from the phone's own text-to-speech engine, such as Speech Services by Google. Niko Clock only gives it the sentence to say. What the engine does is covered by its maker's privacy policy.

</Question>

</Section>

<Section id="accessibility" title="Accessibility">

Version 0.4.2 has been tried by hand on a Xiaomi phone and on the Android emulator, and tested on a Samsung Galaxy A01 at normal and 200% font size. Full testing comes before version 1.0. [How Niko apps are built and tested](/accessibility/).

<Question q="What hasn't been tested yet?">

- A full TalkBack pass of every screen, including setup and the PIN screens.
- 200% font size on more phones and every widget size.
- An Accessibility Scanner pass.
- Everyday use by people who can't read, blind people and older people.
- A native speaker review of the phrases in every language except English and Nepali. Bengali and Urdu need it most: their numbers are written by hand.

</Question>

<Question q="Are there known problems?">

- The settings gear, back arrow, backspace and the (i) buttons in settings show only an icon. Each has a TalkBack label.
- On the smallest widget size with very large text, the date can be cut short.
- Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. The app warns about this, but can't always detect the phone maker's own settings.

</Question>

</Section>

<Section id="questions" title="Questions">

<Question q="It stopped saying the time by itself. What do I do?">

The phone is most likely stopping it to save battery. Set Niko Clock's battery use to "No restrictions" or "Unrestricted". [Step by step for each phone](/install/#battery).

</Question>

<Question q="It says a voice is missing.">

The phone has no voice installed for that language. Install or update Speech Services by Google from the Play Store, then add the language in the phone's text-to-speech settings. Niko Clock won't speak another language instead.

</Question>

<Question q="Can it stay quiet at night?">

Yes. Set quiet hours in settings. Chimes also stop during Do Not Disturb. Tapping or shaking still says the time.

</Question>

<Question q="Does it need the internet?">

No. It has no internet permission and works fully offline.

</Question>

</Section>
