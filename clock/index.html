Niko Clock

Hear the time out loud.

For people who can't easily read a screen: people who can't read, older people and people with low vision. Tap the clock, shake the phone, or let it say the time every hour.

Download for Android

Version 0.2.0, test release. Android 8.0 or newer.

What it does

  • Tap to hear the time. Tap the clock or press the big button. Tap twice for the date.
  • Shake to hear it. Works with the screen off and the phone locked. Shake again for the date.
  • Chimes. Says the time by itself every hour, half hour or quarter hour, with its own volume.
  • Quiet at night. No chimes during quiet hours or Do Not Disturb. Asking still works.
  • Tiles and a widget. Hear the time or the date from quick settings or the home screen.
  • Speaks like a person. "It's quarter to four in the afternoon", not "fifteen forty-five".
  • Set up by a helper. Language, speed, pitch, text size and chimes sit behind an optional PIN.

Languages

Speaks: English, नेपाली, हिन्दी, Español, Português.

Screen: English, नेपाली, हिन्दी, বাংলা, اردو, العربية, Español, Português, Français, Bahasa Indonesia.

It uses the phone's own voice, so it works offline. If a voice is missing, it says so instead of speaking another language.

Install

  1. On the phone, open the latest release and download the APK file.
  2. Open the file. If the phone asks, allow installing apps from this source.
  3. Open Niko Clock and tap the time to hear it.

To get updates automatically, add https://github.com/NikoApps/Clock to Obtainium.

On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung and similar phones, set Niko Clock's battery use to "No restrictions" so chimes and shake keep working.

Privacy

Niko Clock has no internet permission. It collects nothing, shows no ads and has no account. Settings stay on the phone.
